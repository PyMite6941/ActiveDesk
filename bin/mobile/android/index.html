ActiveDesk

Remote control your Windows laptop and Raspberry Pi from your Android phone. Private, peer-to-peer over Tailscale—no server, no cloud.

Install on your Android phone

  1. Install the Tailscale app from Google Play and sign in with the same account as your computers.
  2. Open Chrome and go to your device’s ActiveDesk address, e.g. http://100.x.y.z:47801.
  3. Enter the 8-digit PIN shown when ActiveDesk started on your computer.
  4. Tap the three-dot menu () then choose “Add to Home screen”.
Open ActiveDesk Get Tailscale for Android
No Google Play needed for ActiveDesk itself. It is a web app—once added to your Home Screen it works like a native app, with its own icon. Keep Tailscale running in the background so you can reach your computers from anywhere.
